OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATION

Trading fees

EscapeHub documents a 1.2% bonding-curve trading fee: 0.5% for creators and 0.7% for the protocol. Rates change after graduation.

OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATION

Creator choices

Creators can retain their share, divide it between wallets, or redirect it to traders as cashback.

OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATION

Whitelisted launches

The whitelist is created with the token and enforced on-chain. Its membership is fixed; the creator may open trading permanently, and graduation also makes trading public.
